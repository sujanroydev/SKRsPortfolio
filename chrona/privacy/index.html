Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Chrona processes and how that information is handled.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. About Chrona

Chrona is a Windows desktop application designed to help you understand your laptop usage by tracking active application usage and related activity on your computer.

Chrona is developed and maintained by Sujan Roy.

2. Information Chrona Processes

Depending on the features you use, Chrona may process information about your computer activity, including:

  • Application or process names.
  • Time spent using applications.
  • Dates and times associated with usage.
  • Daily usage totals.

This information is used to provide Chrona's usage tracking and reporting features.

3. Local Storage

Chrona is designed to store usage information locally on your Windows computer. The application does not require an account or cloud service to provide its core tracking functionality.

Usage information stored locally by Chrona remains on your computer unless you choose to copy, share, or otherwise transfer it yourself.

4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Chrona does not intentionally collect the contents of files, documents, messages, passwords, keystrokes, or the content of websites you visit.

Chrona's application tracking is intended to identify the applications being used and measure usage time, not to record the content of your activity.

5. Network Communication

Chrona's core usage-tracking functionality is designed to work locally on your computer and does not require you to create an online account.

If future versions of Chrona introduce features that require network communication or additional data collection, this privacy policy will be updated to explain those changes.

6. Third-Party Services

Chrona may be distributed through third-party platforms such as the Microsoft Store or GitHub. Those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies when you use their websites or services.

Chrona's use of third-party distribution platforms does not mean that Chrona receives all information collected by those platforms.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Usage data created by Chrona is stored on your computer. You can remove Chrona and its locally stored application data using the available Windows controls or by otherwise deleting the relevant application data.

Because Chrona's core usage data is stored locally, Sujan Roy does not maintain a central copy of that usage history.

8. Children's Privacy

Chrona is a general-purpose desktop application and is not specifically directed at children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Chrona's functionality, data practices, or distribution methods change.

The latest version will always be published on this page, together with the date of the most recent update.
